Schema relazionale normalizzato per un sistema di gestione delle iscrizioni universitarie, conforme alla 3NF.

TABLE students
  - student_id (INTEGER)  [PK, NN]
  - student_email (TEXT)  [UQ, NN]
  - student_name (TEXT)  [NN]

TABLE courses
  - course_code (TEXT)  [PK, NN]
  - course_title (TEXT)  [NN]
  - credits (INTEGER)  [NN]

TABLE enrollments
  - enrollment_id (INTEGER)  [PK, NN]
  - student_id (INTEGER)  [NN, FK->students.student_id]
  - course_code (TEXT)  [NN, FK->courses.course_code]
  - semester (TEXT)  [NN]
  - grade (TEXT)  [NN]

RELATIONSHIPS
  - students.student_id [one_to_many] enrollments.student_id
  - courses.course_code [one_to_many] enrollments.course_code
