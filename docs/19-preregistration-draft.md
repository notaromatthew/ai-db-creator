## Research questions and estimands

- **RQ0:** effect of AI + Interface versus Manual on final database quality for non-experts. Primary estimand: intention-to-treat mean difference in blinded expert schema-quality composite.
- **RQ1:** quality of LLM-generated schemas. Primary estimand: mean expert-rated 3NF score by generation condition; relationship F1 and deterministic constraint coverage are secondary.
- **RQ2:** accuracy of automatically populated content. Primary estimand: canonical fact micro-F1 per run, aggregated across frozen datasets. Strict physical-cell fidelity, precision, recall, source-type strata and functional workload results are secondary.
- **RQ3:** effect of validation UI. Primary estimand: AI + Interface versus AI-Only difference in residual schema-error count. Raw NASA-TLX and SUS are key secondary outcomes.
- **RQ4:** association between preregistered interaction features and final schema improvement within AI + Interface. This is observational within a randomised arm; associations are not causal effects.

## Design and allocation

Three-arm, between-subjects experiment: Manual, AI-Only, AI + Interface. Proposed confirmatory allocation is 1:1:1 using a computer-generated sequence, stratified by dataset and self-reported prior database experience (`none` versus `some`). This remains a required protocol decision: the current software implements only deterministic HMAC allocation and must not be described as randomised or stratified. Block sizes and seed would be generated by a researcher who does not rate outcomes and withheld until database lock. Participants, facilitators and analysts cannot be blinded to interface; artifact identifiers given to experts omit condition and participant identity.

Eligibility: adults able to provide consent, target-language proficiency sufficient for instructions, and no professional database-administration role in the preceding two years. Exact criteria, recruitment channel, compensation and jurisdiction are completed before ethics submission. One person may participate once.

| Exploratory | RQ4 | event features, transitions and sequence profiles | participant/session | within Interface arm |

The schema composite is computed only if at least four of five dimensions are available; otherwise it is missing. Dimension scores remain separately reported. RQ2 uses source-grounded canonical facts `(entity, natural_identity, attribute_or_relation, canonical_value)`. Synthetic identifiers are excluded and synthetic foreign keys are resolved to natural identities. A wrong value contributes one FP and one FN; a missing entity contributes all of its gold facts as FN; extra facts contribute FP. Unmatched gold structures remain in the recall denominator.

## Hypotheses and confirmatory contrasts

- H0-RQ0: no difference in schema-quality composite between Interface and Manual.
- H0-RQ3: no difference in residual errors between Interface and AI-Only.
- H0-RQ2: no difference in canonical fact micro-F1 between full-LLM and baseline under the frozen model configuration.

All tests are two-sided. The two human-study primary contrasts form one family and use Holm correction at family-wise alpha .05. RQ2 is a separate technical benchmark family. Dimension-level RQ1 and questionnaire tests are secondary and use Holm correction within their stated family. Exploratory RQ4 p-values use Benjamini–Hochberg FDR and are labelled exploratory; emphasis is on effect sizes and uncertainty.

## Sample size and power assumptions

Sample size is **not yet selected**. The roadmap's original planning scenario is 34 complete participants per arm (102 total) and approximately 114 recruited for 10% attrition, based on `d = 0.70`, two-sided alpha .05 and power .80. A more conservative scenario that allocates alpha .025 to each of two primary contrasts gives approximately 39 complete participants per arm (117 total) and approximately 130 recruited. These are alternative planning assumptions, not targets or evidence. Before registration, reproduce the calculation with named software, decide whether Holm's gatekeeping or conservative per-contrast alpha governs power, document shared-arm correlation assumptions, and freeze one target. A validated pilot variance may inform that decision only before confirmatory outcomes are inspected.

RQ2 uses repeated stochastic runs, not participant power. Freeze at least 20 successful runs per dataset × condition for distributional summaries, while retaining every attempted run and its failure status. Dataset is treated as a crossed design factor, not three independent replications of the same domain.

## Failures, exclusions and deviations

Use intention-to-treat as primary: analyse participants in assigned arm when a final artifact exists, regardless of compliance. Exclude only duplicate enrolment, ineligibility discovered after enrolment, withdrawal requiring deletion, corrupted identity/allocation record, or no task exposure. Do not exclude for low quality, slow performance, unfamiliarity or protocol noncompliance. Report counts and reasons by arm before outcomes.

System/provider failure is an outcome and protocol event. If no artifact can be produced because of a verified infrastructure outage, mark `technical_failure`, retain allocation and timing, and include it in completion-rate analysis. Artifact outcomes are missing and handled below. Benchmark runs that fail remain in the attempted-run denominator; report success rate and analyse quality among successful runs as a conditional secondary result. Never silently rerun or replace a failed run.

## Missing data

Report missingness by variable, arm and reason. No single-value imputation. Primary continuous outcomes use a likelihood-based model under missing-at-random assumptions when defensible; complete-case analysis and multiple imputation using assignment, dataset, experience and observed process variables are sensitivity analyses. Do not impute SUS or NASA-TLX if scoring requirements are not met. Best/worst plausible bounds for technical failures are reported for task success and primary quality. Human-rating gaps are handled by the composite rule above and rater models where applicable.

## Analysis commitments

The frozen statistical analysis plan is `docs/27-statistical-analysis-plan-draft.md`. Report estimates, 95% confidence intervals, adjusted and unadjusted p-values, standardized effect sizes, distribution plots and all denominators. Check model assumptions with residual diagnostics; use robust standard errors or prespecified ordinal/count alternatives rather than selecting a test based only on a preliminary significance test.

No post-hoc selection of model, prompt, provider, temperature, seed, dataset version, alignment or run subset is allowed for the confirmatory result. The primary temperature is selected before confirmatory generation. Other temperatures are sensitivity analyses and are never substituted because they perform better. Existing temperature 0.1/0.5 results remain labelled exploratory.

## Human and LLM adjudication

Semantic alignments not resolved deterministically are decided by two independent reviewers blinded to condition and scores, with a third adjudicator for disagreement. The versioned decision table is frozen and reused across runs. LLM-as-judge scores are exploratory only, cannot determine alignment or primary outcomes, and are never averaged with deterministic or human scores.

## Registration amendments and stopping

No optional stopping based on outcome results. Recruitment ends at the frozen target or documented calendar/resource limit. Every amendment records timestamp, rationale, affected outcomes, whether data were inspected, and approval status. Post-data amendments are clearly separated from preregistered analyses.
