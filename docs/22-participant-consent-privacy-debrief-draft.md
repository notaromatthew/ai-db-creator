## Consent checklist

Use separate unticked confirmations:

- I have read the information sheet and had an opportunity to ask questions.
- I understand participation is voluntary and I may stop without penalty.
- I understand what task, interaction, artifact and questionnaire data are recorded.
- I will use only the supplied study documents and will not enter personal/confidential data.
- I understand pseudonymisation is not the same as anonymity.
- I understand the withdrawal deadline and deletion limits after anonymised aggregation: `[details]`.
- I consent to participate. `[required]`
- I consent to reuse of de-identified data/materials in future research. `[optional and separable]`

Record consent version and timestamp separately from analytic data. Do not use pre-checked boxes.

## Privacy notice fields

Data controller, DPO contact, lawful basis, data categories, purposes, recipients/processors, transfer safeguards, storage region, retention schedule, access controls, rights and complaint authority must be completed with institutional advice. State whether automated decisions occur (proposed: no consequential automated decision). Define the withdrawal authentication process without storing unnecessary identity in research logs.

## Debrief

Thank the participant and explain that the study compares Manual, AI-Only and AI + Interface workflows. Do not claim one is expected to win. Explain that model outputs can vary and may be incorrect even when the database appears plausible. Reiterate contact, withdrawal deadline, complaint route and compensation. Ask participants not to share task details until recruitment closes, without limiting their right to report concerns.

## Facilitator safety script

For distress, accidental personal-data entry, suspected credential exposure or application incident: stop the task, preserve only the minimum incident metadata, do not copy exposed content into logs, contact the approved incident lead and follow the institution’s reporting procedure. This draft does not define or replace that procedure.
