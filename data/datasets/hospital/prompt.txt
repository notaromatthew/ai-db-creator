Design a fully normalized relational schema (3NF) for a hospital management system.

Source documents describe: patients, doctors, wards, medications, appointments,
treatments, prescriptions linking treatments to medications, and invoices
referencing appointments. Department-to-ward mapping and billing rules are
described in the operational notes.

Infer tables, primary keys, foreign keys, column names, types, nullability and
uniqueness constraints from the attached documents. Produce a clean relational
schema ready to be populated.
